# Founder-Led Delivery Playbook

How one person with a W2 delivers excellent audits without breaking either commitment.
Time budget per audit: **10–12 founder-hours across two weeks.** Five founding clients,
staggered — never more than two audits in flight.

## Weekly operating rhythm (during validation)

| Slot | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Early mornings (Tu/Th 7–8:30a) | Outreach + follow-ups (owners read email then) |
| 2 lunch blocks | Fit calls (20 min each, buffer included) |
| 1 evening block (~2h) | Analysis/writing for active audit |
| Sat morning (2–3h) | Deep work: scoring, deliverable drafting |
| Sun (15 min) | Pipeline review in `/admin` vs. 30-day targets |

Calendly exposes ONLY the fit-call slots and interview slots. Never freeform-schedule.

## The audit, hour by hour

**Stage 0 — Fit call (0.5h)** → run `DISCOVERY_SCRIPT.md`; log notes + stage same day.
**Stage 1 — Sold (0.5h):** invoice (100% upfront) + intake link + interview booking in one
email. Stage → `audit_paid`.
**Stage 2 — Intake review (1h):** read everything in `/admin`; pre-score opportunities as
hypotheses; write 5 interview probes the intake can't answer. Stage auto-advances on submission.
**Stage 3 — Owner interview (1.5h + 0.5h same-day notes):** their office if possible. Follow
the time, not your hypotheses: "walk me through last Tuesday." Capture verbatim quotes — they
make readouts land. Log as `discovery_notes` (owner_interview) BEFORE sleeping.
**Stage 4 — Analysis (3–4h, the Saturday block):** score all opportunities in `/admin` (the
total formula does the ranking); data-risk table; draft the deliverable from
`AI_OPERATING_AUDIT_DELIVERABLE_TEMPLATE.md`; build mockup ONLY if it changes the top
recommendation's clarity (cap: 1 hour, dummy data, watermarked).
**Stage 5 — Readout (1.5h):** walk the document, don't read it. Order: snapshot → where your
time goes (quotes) → the map → top 5 → roadmap → what NOT to automate → commercials, last and
unhurried. Then the referral ask (`REFERRAL_SCRIPT.md` #3). Stage → `audit_delivered`; create
the proposal in `/admin` with the $500 credit line item; 14-day validity.
**Stage 6 — Follow-up:** day 3 ("questions after sitting with it?") and day 10 ("credit
expires ⟨date⟩ — want it?"). Two touches, then a task for a quarter-later check-in. No chasing.

## Quickstart delivery (2 weeks, ~15–20 founder-hours)

Days 1–2 scope-lock (workflow, data boundaries, review points, "done means") · 3–7 build with
their real scrubbed examples · 8–10 team testing (the people who'll run it, not the owner) ·
11–14 SOP + walkthrough + handoff. Then two weeks of async adjustment (batch to 2 check-ins).
**Scope discipline:** new asks mid-build → "great instinct — that's workflow #2; let's get
this one earning first." Write it on the roadmap so it's captured, not rejected.

## Quality bars (non-negotiable even at $950)

Every deliverable: owner-readable in 30 minutes · at least one "do NOT automate this yet" ·
data-risk table present · every score defensible aloud · zero hype vocabulary (check against
`docs/SALES_COPY.md` banned list) · proofread once out loud.

## Capacity tripwires

>2 audits in flight → stop selling, deliver. Quickstart slipping past 3 weeks → cut scope to
the SOP-able core, never extend silently. W2 conflict week → move the readout BEFORE the
client notices drift; "I'm moving us to Thursday to do this properly" is a premium behavior.

## Tooling discipline

`/admin` is the single source of truth — if it isn't logged there, it didn't happen. Notes
same-day, always. Deliverables drafted in your doc tool, linked via `deliverables.link_url`.
Client files NEVER in the app during validation (no upload path exists by design — that's a
feature).
