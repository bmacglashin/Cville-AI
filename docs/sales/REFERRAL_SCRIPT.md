# Referral Scripts

Referrals are the entire founding-cohort engine. Five warm intros beat fifty cold emails in a
town the size of Charlottesville. Ask at the moment of goodwill — readouts, "not a fit" calls,
chamber breakfasts.

## 1. The warm-network ask (friends, Darden, former colleagues)

> "Quick one — I've launched my AI advisory practice here in Charlottesville. I run paid
> operating audits for owner-led businesses: design/build, remodeling, premium home services —
> the kind where the owner is still the unofficial COO and every proposal waits on them.
>
> I'm taking five founding clients at $950 (goes to $1,250 after). **Who's the one business
> owner you know who's drowning in their own operation?** I just need a name — I'll do the
> work, and I'll mention you only if you're comfortable."

*Ask for ONE owner. "Anyone you know?" gets zero; "who's the one?" gets a name.*

## 2. After a fit call that wasn't a fit

> "Like I said, I don't think the audit earns its keep for you right now. Different question:
> who do you know who's buried — proposals stacking up, team interrupting them all day? That's
> exactly who I built this for, and I have founding pricing for ⟨n⟩ more businesses."

## 3. After an audit readout (the strongest moment — use it every time)

> "Before we wrap: this is a referral business, and you've now seen exactly what the audit is.
> Who are the two owners in your world who need this? Suppliers, subs, the GC you swap
> referrals with, whoever's across the table at chamber breakfasts. If either books an audit,
> I'll add a working session to your engagement at no charge."

## 4. The connector ask (bankers, CPAs, attorneys, chamber staff)

> "You see inside more local businesses than anyone. I'm not asking you to endorse me — I'm
> asking: when an owner tells you they're drowning in operations and asks about AI, who do you
> send them to today? If the answer is 'nobody good,' I'd like to be the safe answer. Here's my
> one-pager; the audit is paid and scoped, so it's a low-risk thing to recommend."

## 5. The email version (forwardable — send AFTER a verbal yes)

> Subject: the AI audit thing I mentioned
>
> ⟨Name⟩ — as promised, the forwardable version:
>
> I run Agent Ally, a Charlottesville AI advisory practice for owner-led businesses. I start
> with a paid AI Operating Audit ($950 founding price): two weeks, a 90-minute owner interview,
> and a written answer to "where would AI actually help my business — and where would it waste
> money or create risk." Includes a scored use-case map and a 30-day roadmap. No hype, no
> chatbots, no regulated data, human review on everything.
>
> If that sounds like someone you know: ⟨site⟩/apply — or just reply and I'll take it from there.
> — Ben

## Logging

Every referral → `/admin/leads` with `source` noted, or a `contacts` entry on the referring
org. Thank the referrer the same day; tell them the outcome (win or lose) — that's what makes
them refer twice.
