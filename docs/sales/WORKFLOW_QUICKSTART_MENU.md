# Workflow Quickstart Menu

The seven workflows we build, with scoping notes for the advisor. Public versions live on
`/quickstart`; this version adds delivery guidance. **One Quickstart = one workflow** — when a
client wants two, sequence them; never blend scopes.

**Every Quickstart includes:** scope-lock session · build on scrubbed/approved data only ·
human-review checkpoints · team testing · written SOP · owner walkthrough · 2 weeks of
post-launch adjustment. **Founding $3,500 · standard $4,500–$5,000** (price the high end when
corpus prep is heavy).

---

## 1. Proposal assistant ⭐ (most common first build)
- **For:** design/build, remodeling, premium trades
- **Does:** drafts proposals/estimates from scrubbed past projects + encoded pricing rules;
  owner reviews and sends every one
- **Needs:** 15–25 past proposals (client-name scrub first), pricing-rule interview
- **Done means:** 3 real proposals drafted→reviewed→sent; drafting time cut ≥60%
- **Watch out:** the assistant DRAFTS, it never PRICES — say this in the SOP and the readout

## 2. Internal SOP assistant
- **For:** any owner-led team ≥6 people
- **Does:** answers staff questions from approved SOPs only; logs unanswered questions into a
  weekly gap report (half the value is the gap report)
- **Needs:** existing SOPs however messy; 2–3 staff testers
- **Done means:** team answers 10 benchmark questions without the owner; gap report producing
- **Watch out:** garbage corpus = confident garbage; budget the consolidation pass

## 3. Customer inquiry triage drafts
- **For:** high-inquiry-volume services
- **Does:** classifies inbound inquiries (type/urgency/fit) and drafts replies for human review.
  **No auto-send, ever.**
- **Needs:** shared inbox (not a personal Gmail), 50+ historical inquiries for tone
- **Done means:** first-touch turnaround under 1 business day with human approval intact
- **Watch out:** if email infrastructure is a mess, fix that first — say so in the audit

## 4. Project handoff checklist generator
- **For:** design/build, property management
- **Does:** turns project parameters into stage-specific handoff checklists with named sign-offs
- **Needs:** 2–3 past handoffs (incl. one that went wrong), production lead co-design
- **Done means:** next two real handoffs run on generated checklists; zero "I thought you told them"
- **Watch out:** must be co-owned by the production lead or it dies in a drawer

## 5. Owner weekly priorities digest
- **For:** owners who are the unofficial COO
- **Does:** Monday brief from approved sources — stalled proposals, follow-up gaps, handoff
  risks, top-3 actions (see `SAMPLE_OWNER_WEEKLY_OPS_BRIEF.md`)
- **Needs:** the underlying trackers to exist (often workflows #1–#4 first) — usually a
  SECOND Quickstart, not a first
- **Done means:** 4 consecutive Mondays of briefs the owner actually reads (<4 min)
- **Watch out:** don't sell this first to a business with no structured sources; it'll be hollow

## 6. Staff onboarding knowledge base
- **For:** teams 6–40 with hiring plans
- **Does:** organizes scattered training material into a structured path + Q&A assistant
  (approved content only)
- **Needs:** training materials audit; one upcoming hire to pilot on
- **Done means:** new hire reaches "useful" with measurably fewer owner interruptions
- **Watch out:** scope-creeps into SOP assistant — decide which one it is at scope-lock

## 7. Review & content draft system
- **For:** reputation-driven local businesses
- **Does:** drafts review responses, project write-ups, follow-up notes in the owner's voice —
  human approves everything that posts
- **Needs:** 20+ examples of the owner's writing, review-platform access
- **Done means:** weekly cadence sustained a month with <15 min/week of owner time
- **Watch out:** voice-matching matters more than volume; over-posting reads as fake

## Picking the first workflow (decision rule)

Highest `total_score` from the audit usually wins, but break ties with: **(1)** which workflow
the TEAM will adopt fastest (visible early win beats theoretical ROI), **(2)** which needs the
least data prep, **(3)** which the owner will personally use weekly. Never start with a
workflow whose data scored <3 on readiness.
