## The sentence

Agent Ally is a **tool-agnostic AI operating partner** for owner-led businesses. We audit the

system, train the team, and stay on to operate and improve it.

**Your accounts. Your data. Our operating method.**

## Existing tools first

Most of what AI can do for an owner-led business today runs inside tools you already pay for —
your workspace suite, a business-grade AI assistant, a knowledge base, an automation platform.
So that's where we start. It's faster, cheaper, easier to staff, and easier to leave. We use
the right tool, not one tool: recommendations are made case-by-case from the whole market,
vetted against our data-risk framework, and set up in accounts your business owns. We don't
resell software, we don't take vendor commissions, and we never white-label someone else's
product as ours.

## When we build custom

Rarely, and never first. A custom build (typically a small Supabase/Next.js application)
happens only when all four are true:

1. **ROI** — the audit shows real, recurring value off-the-shelf tools can't capture.
2. **Data risk** — the data involved is non-regulated and the boundaries are designed first.
3. **Scope** — it's a focused piece of glue or a point application, not "a platform."
4. **Budget** — the owner sees the number and still says yes, under its own paid SOW.

Custom is always post-audit and always its own statement of work. Most clients never need it,
and we say so.

## Managed AI Workspace vs. a custom app

The **Managed AI Workspace** ($8,500 founding setup / $12,500 standard) is *not software we
build*. It's your proven workflows — usually after one or more Quickstarts — configured across
your existing tools into one documented operating stack: SOPs, prompt library, data boundaries,
human-review rules, team training. Not a private platform; a documented way of running AI in
tools you already own. A custom app is the rare exception above, and the audit is what tells
us which (if either) your business justifies.

## What the Operate retainer includes (from $1,500/month)

A monthly operating cadence, run by the person who built the system: workflow health checks,
adoption review, output-quality sampling, risk check (data boundaries + vendor changes),
prompt/SOP updates, backlog refresh, a one-page owner brief, and training notes. Full cadence:
`docs/OPERATE_RETAINER_PLAYBOOK.md`. Cancel with 30 days' notice — everything keeps working,
because everything is in your accounts.

## What we will not automate

No exceptions, no price: health/PHI workflows, student records, tenant screening, employment/
credit/lending/insurance decisions, legal or tax advice, payroll decisioning, financial
credentials, children's data — and nothing customer-facing, financial, or judgment-heavy
without a person reviewing it. When a workflow touches those areas, the recommendation reads
"decline or defer," and that honesty is part of what the audit is for.
