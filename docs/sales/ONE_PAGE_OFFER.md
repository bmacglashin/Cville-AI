## A tool-agnostic AI operating partner for Charlottesville owner-led businesses.

I'm not selling a platform. I help owner-led businesses figure out **which workflows are worth
improving with AI, which tools are safe to use, and what to implement first** — then I set it
up in accounts you own, document it, train your team, and (if you want) stay on to operate it.

**Your accounts. Your data. My operating method.**

### The AI Operating Audit — $950 *(founding price; standard $1,250)*

Two weeks. Delivered personally by Ben. You get:

- A **90-minute owner interview** — where your real operating model lives
- A **tool & process inventory** of how work actually flows
- An **AI opportunity map** with every use case scored: impact, effort, risk, data-readiness
- A **data-risk ranking** — what's safe to use, what's excluded (regulated data is always excluded)
- A **recommended tool stack** — existing tools first, every account owned by your business
- Your **top 5 use cases**, a **30-day roadmap**, and what we'd *decline or defer* (in writing)
- Where useful, a **small mockup** built on dummy data so you can react to something real

**$500 is credited toward implementation** if we proceed together within 14 days — so the audit
effectively costs $450 if we build. And if the honest answer is "AI isn't ready for your
operation yet," you get that in writing, with what would change it.

### Then, if — and only if — the ROI justifies it:

**AI Workflow Quickstart — $3,500** *(founding; standard $4,500–$5,000)*
One workflow from your roadmap, working in tools you already own, in two weeks. Human-review
checkpoints on anything customer-facing. A written SOP so your team runs it without me.

**Managed AI Workspace + Operate — $8,500 setup** *(founding; standard $12,500)*, retainer from
$1,500/month. Your proven workflows documented into one operating stack across your own tools —
not a private platform — with a monthly cadence keeping it healthy. Post-audit only.

*Example workflows: proposal assistant · internal SOP assistant · inquiry triage drafts ·
project handoff checklists · owner weekly priorities digest*

### The ground rules (in writing, always)

**Existing tools first — custom builds only when the business case is clear.** We use the right
tool, not one tool; specific tools are recommended case-by-case and remain subject to vendor
terms, data requirements, and your approval. No regulated data — health, student, lending,
personnel files. No AI acting without a person reviewing it. No reselling software, no vendor
kickbacks, no hype — if a process fix beats a model, the audit says so.

### Who this is for

Design/build, remodeling, architecture-adjacent, and premium home-service businesses (5–40
people) where the owner is still the unofficial COO. Also a fit: property managers, venues,
owner-led professional services.

### Who you're working with

Ben — Darden MBA, former Deloitte consultant leading go-to-market AI work, hands-on AI builder,
Charlottesville resident. **Founding cohort is capped at five businesses** because one person
delivers everything.
