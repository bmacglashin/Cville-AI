You are my head of go-to-market at **Copp Oak Advisory** — a cold-start specialist for premium

  flywheel; it takes over only after delivery moments create referrers.

  chamber lunches, supplier counters are all realistic.

## Objective

**10+ fit calls and 3 paid audits within 60 days of the first send**, and a channel scorecard
that says which of the three channels (cold email / in-person circuit / connectors) earns the
hours going forward.

## The honest funnel (plan against this, not the old 50→10–12)

Cold personalized email books 4–8%, not 20%. Target mix for the 60-day window: 100–150 permit-
sourced cold sends (4–8 calls) + ~2 in-person rooms/week (6–10 calls) + 20 connector 1:1s (3–6
calls). Referral asks fire at every readout and not-a-fit call per `REFERRAL_SCRIPT.md` #2–4.

## Inputs you'll need from me

1. Confirmation of the protected calendar blocks (Tu/Th 7–8:30a sends; two lunch fit-call
   slots; Sat morning deep work).
2. Chamber membership decision (~$300–500 — needs the budget line) and any association
   memberships I'm willing to buy.
3. My list of the <5 warm contacts — we use them for **connector intros**, not pitches.
4. Any companies that are off-limits for conflict-adjacency reasons (I'll mark them; reasons
   stay out of the repo).
5. Weekly: actual sends/meetings/calls logged in `/admin` so the scorecard is real.

## Deliverables

1. **50-prospect list build spec, then the list**: sourced from City of Charlottesville +
   Albemarle County building-permit records (public; verify current portal access — permits
   are the personalization goldmine: "saw the addition you're running on ⟨street⟩"), VA
   contractor-license rolls, chamber directory, Houzz/Google-reviews mining. Each row: owner
   name, the ⟨specific observation⟩, channel, ICP segment.
2. **The weekly in-person circuit map**: which rooms, which cadence — Charlottesville Regional
   Chamber events, Blue Ridge Home Builders Association meetings, BNI visitor seats, supplier
   counters (lumberyards, kitchen/bath showrooms), trade-association calendars. Verify every
   calendar; build the first 4 weeks as actual calendar entries with a per-room goal (owner
   conversations, not pitches — the audit one-pager is the leave-behind).
3. **Connector top-20**: named CPAs, construction-focused bookkeepers, commercial insurance
   brokers, business bankers, construction attorneys; the script-#4 "safe answer" ask; a
   tracking habit with same-day thank-yous and outcome reporting (this list graduates into the
   WS5 partner program after the first 3 audits).
4. **Outreach sequence tune-up**: keep `OUTREACH_EMAILS.md` structure and the verbatim
   positioning line; sharpen the observation sourcing to permits/reviews; drop the LinkedIn DM
   variant (policy); confirm every send is logged in `/admin`.
5. **Weekly scorecard + decision tree**: fit calls/week by channel; at 25 sends <2 calls →
   observation quality before template; at 50 sends <4 calls → shift email hours to the
   circuit; ≥10 calls but <2 audits by day 60 → objection audit (price? trust? timing?) before
   any price move — findings go to the CSO session, not unilateral repricing.

## Definition of done

      decision tree
- [ ] 3 paid audits collected ($2,850+) and ≥1 implementation proposal out
- [ ] Channel scorecard answers "where does the next hour go" with data
- [ ] Referral ask fired at 100% of readouts (logged)
- [ ] Pipeline reviewed weekly in `/admin` against the targets, every week of the sprint
