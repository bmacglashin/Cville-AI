# Sales Copy & Message Bank

The positioning system behind the website. Use these blocks verbatim in emails, the one-pager,
LinkedIn, and conversation. Field assets live in `docs/sales/`.

## Positioning

**Primary:** Practical AI implementation for Charlottesville owner-led businesses.

**Sharp (ICP):** AI Operating Audits and Workflow Quickstarts for design/build, remodeling,
architecture-adjacent, and premium home-service businesses where the owner is still the
unofficial COO.

**One-breath version:** "I run paid AI audits for owner-led businesses in Charlottesville —
two weeks, and you know exactly which one workflow is worth automating, what it'll save you,
and what to keep away from AI entirely."

fewer owner interruptions · faster proposals · cleaner handoffs · better follow-up · fewer

## Never lead with (banned vocabulary)

## Core messages (the four load-bearing sentences)

1. "Most local businesses do not need more AI tools. They need judgment, prioritization, and
   one practical workflow that removes real operational drag."
2. "Start with the audit. Build only where the ROI and risk profile justify it."
3. "AI should reduce owner dependency, not create another system to babysit."
4. "We start with a paid audit so the work is serious from day one — including when the honest
   answer is 'not yet.'"

## Objection responses

**"Why would I pay for an assessment?"**
"Because free AI assessments are sales calls wearing a costume. You're paying for the answer —
scored use cases, a data-risk ranking, a 30-day plan — and it's yours whether or not we ever
work together again. If the answer is 'AI isn't ready for your shop yet,' you'll get that in
writing too, with what would change it."

**"We already use ChatGPT."**
"That's typical — a couple of people, no policy, results that depend on who's typing. The audit
isn't about access to AI; it's about which workflow is worth systematizing, what data it can
safely touch, and an SOP so it works the same on Marcus's worst day as on his best."

**"Is my data safe?"**
"The first thing the audit does is rank your data by sensitivity, and the early work runs on
dummy data or documents you explicitly approve. Health records, personnel files, anything
regulated — excluded outright; it's in writing on the website. And nothing customer-facing goes
out without a person approving it."

**"How are you different from an automation agency?"**
"Agencies sell installs. I sell judgment first — Darden MBA, AI strategy work at Deloitte, and
I build these workflows myself. Five founding clients, delivered personally, audit before
anything. If an agency is the right answer for you, the audit will say so."

**"Can we skip to the build?"**
"No — and that's for your protection as much as mine. Building before auditing is how owners
end up with $20k of shelf-ware. The audit is two weeks and $950; if the build is obvious, it'll
still be obvious in two weeks, and $500 of the audit rides along as credit."
