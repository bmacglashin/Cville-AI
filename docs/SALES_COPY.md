The positioning system behind the website. Use these blocks verbatim in emails, the one-pager,
LinkedIn, and conversation. Field assets live in `docs/sales/`; the doctrine behind the
tool-agnostic stance lives in `docs/TOOL_DOCTRINE.md`.

## Positioning

**Primary:** A tool-agnostic AI operating partner for Charlottesville owner-led businesses.

**Full statement:** Copp Oak Advisory audits the business, recommends the right client-owned tool
stack, configures safe workflows, documents the system, trains the team, and stays on to
operate and improve it. Existing tools first. Custom builds only when ROI, data risk, scope,
and budget justify it.

**Sharp (ICP):** AI Operating Audits and Workflow Quickstarts for design/build, remodeling,
architecture-adjacent, and premium home-service businesses where the owner is still the
unofficial COO.

**One-breath version:** "I'm not selling a platform. I help owner-led businesses figure out
which workflows are worth improving with AI, which tools are safe to use, and what to implement
first — set up in accounts they own."

**The three signature lines (use everywhere):**

1. "Your accounts. Your data. Our operating method."
2. "Existing tools first. Custom builds only when the business case is clear."
3. "We use the right tool, not one tool."

## Objection responses

**"Why would I pay for an assessment?"**
"Because free AI assessments are sales calls wearing a costume. You're paying for the answer —
scored use cases, a data-risk ranking, a recommended tool stack, a 30-day plan — and it's yours
whether or not we ever work together again. If the answer is 'AI isn't ready for your shop
yet,' you'll get that in writing too, with what would change it."

**"We already use ChatGPT."**
"That's typical — a couple of people, no policy, results that depend on who's typing. The audit
isn't about access to AI; it's about which workflow is worth systematizing, which tool tier is
actually safe for your data, and an SOP so it works the same on Marcus's worst day as on his
best."

**"Are you going to sell us your software?"**
"No. I don't have a platform to sell you — that's deliberate. Everything gets configured in
tool accounts your business owns, and you keep all of it if we part ways. What I sell is the
method: knowing which workflows are worth it, which tools are safe, and how to run them. If a
custom build is ever genuinely justified, it gets its own scope and price after the audit — and
most businesses never need one."

**"Is my data safe?"**
"The first thing the audit does is rank your data by sensitivity, and the early work runs on
dummy data or documents you explicitly approve. Every tool we recommend is vetted for business
terms, no-training guarantees, and admin controls — and set up in your accounts, not ours.
Health records, personnel files, anything regulated — excluded outright; it's in writing on the
website. And nothing customer-facing goes out without a person approving it."

**"How are you different from an automation agency?"**
"Agencies sell installs. I sell judgment first — Darden MBA, AI strategy work at Deloitte, and
I build these workflows myself. Five founding clients, delivered personally, audit before
anything, and no incentive to push any particular tool because I don't resell any. If an agency
is the right answer for you, the audit will say so."

**"Can we skip to the build?"**
"No — and that's for your protection as much as mine. Building before auditing is how owners
end up with $20k of shelf-ware. The audit is two weeks and $950; if the build is obvious, it'll
still be obvious in two weeks, and $500 of the audit rides along as credit."
